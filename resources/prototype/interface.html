How to use this prototype

This prototype shows what goes on each page and how people move through the site. It isn’t designed at all: that comes after the prototype is approved.

  1. Follow a journey, or look around. A journey on the left walks you through the site the way a particular visitor would, one step at a time: press Next, or click the link on the page. To look around, click links on the pages, or open any page from the Sitemap tab.
  2. Each page shows twice. On desktop and on mobile. Scroll either one; with “Scroll together” on, the other follows.
  3. Grey boxes and [bracketed words] are placeholders. Photos and writing still to come.
  4. Comment on anything. Press Comments to open them, then Add comment and click the spot you mean on either page. Numbered pins show the comments: 1 a comment, 2 a decision to make, 3 done. Click a pin to read it and reply, or drag it if it covers something; All comments lists every comment on the prototype.
  5. Notes say what a page is for and what’s on it. Press Notes to open them; pages without notes have no button.
  6. The tabs at the top show the whole sitemap and the content behind the site.

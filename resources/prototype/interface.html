How to use this prototype

This prototype shows what goes on each page and how people move through the site. It isn’t the final look: that comes with the design.

  1. Follow a journey, or look around. A journey on the left walks you through the site the way a particular visitor would, one step at a time: press Next, or click the link on the page. To look around, click links on the pages, or open any page from the Sitemap tab.
  2. Each page shows twice. On desktop and on mobile. Scroll either one; with “Scroll together” on, the other follows.
  3. Grey boxes and [bracketed words] are placeholders. Photos and writing still to come.
  4. Numbered circles are decisions. 4 still to decide, 8 decided. Click one to read it.
  5. Page notes say what each page is for and what content it needs. Open them from the strip on the right, or click a numbered circle.
  6. The tabs at the top show the whole sitemap, the content behind the site, and every decision in one list.

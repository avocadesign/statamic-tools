## When to use

- Answering the questions people ask before they get in touch, book or buy, such as prices, how the process works or what happens next.
- A page of frequently asked questions, or a few questions near the end of a service or product page.
- Set Questions to show to Chosen questions to pick the questions that suit the page, in your own order. All questions shows the whole FAQs collection, in collection order or A to Z.

## When not to use

- Something every visitor must read: put it in a Text block, because an answer stays hidden until someone opens its question.
- A long explanation that needs headings, images or tables: use Text or Text Columns, or give it a page of its own, because an answer holds only short text, lists and links.
- Features, benefits or steps of equal weight: use Cards, which shows them side by side.

## Notes for AI

- Questions and answers are entries in the FAQs collection, not text in the block. Add or edit them in the collection, then show them with this block.
- Write each question the way a customer would ask it, and answer it in a short paragraph of about 50 words, or a short list. Answers can hold bold and italic text, lists and links, but no headings.
- Base every answer on facts from the client, such as prices, times and policies. Never guess them.
- Use one FAQ block on a page. When a page needs more questions, choose them all in the same block.
- The block adds FAQPage structured data for the questions it shows, so don't add FAQ JSON-LD in the page's SEO tab as well. Structured data doesn't guarantee a rich result in search.
- A link to the page that ends with # and the question's slug, such as #how-long-does-delivery-take, opens that answer.
- With Chosen questions and none chosen, the block shows every question.
- The preset installs four sample questions about the block itself. Replace them with the site's real questions before launch.
